A

Problem

In 2020 I built an online house bidding website (PHP and MySQL) as my MSc IT final year project. It worked, but bidding felt confusing and risky.

Users could not easily tell the current highest bid, how much time was left, or whether they were winning, the three things that matter most in an auction.

B

What I Designed

I redesigned it as a mobile app in Figma. First I built a reusable design system: 9 colour styles, 6 Inter text styles, an 8-point spacing scale and 12 components such as buttons, timer chips, status badges, a property card and the navigation bar.

Then I designed five screens: Home, Property Details, Live Bidding, an Outbid state and My Bids. I linked them into a clickable prototype. Navy builds trust, orange signals urgency, and green and red show whether you are winning.

Result

Every bidding screen now answers the three key questions at a glance, with one big number, one clear status and one main action.

The project shows my UX thinking: taking a real project of my own, finding its problems and designing a clearer, more trustworthy experience.

Features

Design highlights

  • Live auctions at a glance

    Home shows live and ending-soon properties with price and timer on every card.

  • Trust before bidding

    A verified-seller badge, key features and a clear bid summary on the details screen.

  • One clear action

    Live bidding shows one big number, one status and one main button.

  • Quick-add bids

    Buttons like +1 Lac and +5 Lac make raising a bid fast.

  • Outbid alert

    A red status and a "Bid again" button so users react instantly.

  • My Bids tracker

    Every auction in one place, with Winning or Outbid status and time left.

  • Design system

    9 colour styles, 6 text styles and 12 reusable components.

  • Clickable prototype

    All screens are linked in Figma with smooth animated transitions.

  • Real content

    Realistic prices, timers and names instead of placeholder text.
